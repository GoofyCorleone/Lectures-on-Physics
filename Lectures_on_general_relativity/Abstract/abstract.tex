\section*{Abstract}

    These notes build the theory of general relativity from the ground up. After tracing how our understanding of gravity evolved from the ancient Greeks to Einstein's own revolution, the book develops the differential geometry needed to speak of curved space-time: manifolds, charts, tangent vectors and the metric tensor. With that language in hand, it turns to the theory itself, deriving the geodesic equation, the Riemann curvature tensor and the Einstein field equations that relate the curvature of space-time to the matter and energy within it. The final chapter linearises these field equations around a flat background to obtain the wave equation for gravitational waves, and studies their propagation and polarisation properties.
